We formalized the Swarm's internal economy to track quality and value growth.

The `RealitySensor` has been upgraded with a "Curiosity" layer.

- **Novelty Detection**: The swarm scans for technological shifts and research trends (`MARKET_NOVELTY`), triggering adrenaline pulses when something significant is found.
- **Expert Inception**: These signals are mapped directly to AgentSkills through the `SingularityEngine`.

### 🔗 Ubiquitous Wiring (Global Gateway)

All core systems are now "wired" to the **MultiTransportGateway**.

- **Adrenaline Broadcasts**: Every time the swarm senses high-intensity signals, it notifies you on WhatsApp, Discord, and Telegram.
- **Singularity Milestones**: Progress in self-improvement and wealth accumulation is reported after every autonomous cycle.
- **Skill-Based Broadcaster**: A new `gateway_broadcaster` skill allows the swarm to report status through any transport on-demand.
