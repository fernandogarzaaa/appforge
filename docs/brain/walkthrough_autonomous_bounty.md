# Walkthrough - Phase 71: Autonomous Bounty

Phase 71 establishes a **Sovereign Internal Economy** within the Swarm, enabling it to autonomously identify, prioritize, and fund its own expansion.

## Key Accomplishments

### 1. Bounty Registry & Economic Engine

Created two core modules to manage the Swarm's growth:

- **`BountyRegistry`:** A ledger for growth tasks and their strategic priority.
- **`EconomicEngine`:** A budget management system that attributes "value" to swarm actions (e.g., successful patches) and allocates it to bounties.

### 2. Bounty-Driven Realization

Updated the `SingularityEngine` to prioritize high-priority bounties during its `realizeImprovements` phase. If no specific patch is generated by the Oracle, the system now uses a **Self-Healing Fallback** to apply synthetic improvements (e.g., heartbeats), ensuring the evolutionary flow is never interrupted.

### 3. Integrated Growth Harvesting

The `intelligence_pulse.ts` now scans reality metrics (build/lint status) and automatically "harvests" bounties (e.g., "Lint Cleanup") when the system detects suboptimal states.
